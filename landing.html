AI Customer Support for Small Businesses

A flat-rate AI chatbot that answers customer questions, qualifies leads, and handles FAQs — without usage fees or per-resolution charges that make Zendesk and Intercom too expensive for small teams.

What small businesses pay today:
Zendesk: $19–115+/agent/month
Intercom: $19–132+/seat/month + $0.99 per resolution
Tidio: $24–300/month
Verified prices from vendor websites (Oct 2026). All include unpredictable AI usage fees.

The Problem

Small businesses are priced out of customer-support software:

  • Zendesk and Intercom scale to thousands per year for a 2-person team — far beyond most small business budgets.
  • AI usage fees (per-resolution charges from Intercom Fin, Zendesk Copilot) make bills spike unpredictably — "the better the AI works, the higher the bill."
  • Complex routing rules, shared inboxes, and admin overhead are designed for enterprise — not a shop with one owner handling support themselves.

The Proposed Solution

A simple AI customer-support toolkit built for small businesses:

  • Flat monthly rate — no per-resolution fees, no usage tiers, no surprise bills.
  • Pre-trained for common small-business workflows — FAQs, order lookups, appointment booking, return requests.
  • Multi-channel support — web chat widget, WhatsApp, and email auto-responses in one place.
  • No-code setup — install the widget with a 3-minute copy-paste, train the AI by pasting your FAQs.

Intended Audience

This concept targets:

  • Independent e-commerce stores (Shopify, Daraz.pk) with 1–5 staff.
  • Local service businesses (salons, repair shops, consultants) handling their own customer messages.
  • Small agencies managing support for 2–3 client websites.
Note: This is an early concept, not a launched product. I'm a solo developer validating demand before building. No code has been written yet, no AI model is live, and no customers exist. If enough small-business owners express genuine interest, I'll build an MVP using free-tier APIs (OpenRouter/Ollama) and publish it here. You'll only hear from me if the tool is actually available.
